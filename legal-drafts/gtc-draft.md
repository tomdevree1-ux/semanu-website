## 1. Provider and scope

These GTC govern the subscription services of the **sole proprietorship Tom de Vree**
(Einzelfirma) with its registered seat at Wylerstrasse 75, 3000 Bern, Switzerland,
owner Tom de Vree, email hello@amai.ch ("amai!"). They apply to all website subscription plans
(Starter, Business, Premium) and related services, and prevail over any terms of
the customer unless expressly agreed otherwise in writing.

## 2. Services

amai! builds and operates websites on a monthly subscription. Depending on the
plan this includes design and build, hosting, maintenance, security and SEO
foundations. The exact scope follows the selected plan (see the website) and any
written agreement. The optional "Logo + brand guidelines" add-on is a separate
one-time service.

## 3. Conclusion of contract and start of work

The contract is concluded when the customer subscribes and amai! confirms the
order (day 0). amai! starts work on the design **within 5 working days after the
first payment has been received**. The customer provides the needed content,
access and information in good time.

## 4. Term, renewal and cancellation

- **Minimum term: 24 months** from the start of the subscription.
- After the minimum term the contract **continues on a monthly basis** and may be
  cancelled with **[1 month] notice to the end of a month**.
- Cancellation must be in text form (email to hello@amai.ch is sufficient).

## 5. Termination fees

On termination by the customer for reasons not attributable to amai!, the following
applies:

- **During the minimum term (first 24 months):** the **remaining monthly fees until
  the end of month 24** plus an **early termination fee of CHF 1,250**.
- **After the minimum term, up to 60 months:** an **early termination fee of
  CHF 1,250** (no remaining monthly fees).
- **After 60 months:** a **termination fee of CHF 250**.

Statutory rights to terminate for good cause remain reserved.

## 6. Prices, payment and indexation

- Plan fees are billed **monthly in advance**. Starter 49 CHF/month, Business
  99 CHF/month, Premium by quote. The Logo + brand guidelines add-on is **950 CHF one-time**.
- The first invoice is issued at order (day 0); work starts within 5 working days
  of receipt of payment (§3).
- **Annual indexation:** amai! may adjust prices once per year in line with the
  **Swiss Consumer Price Index (Landesindex der Konsumentenpreise, LIK)** published
  by the Federal Statistical Office (BFS), with prior notice. *(This is the standard
  Swiss index for such clauses.)*
- All prices are in CHF **plus VAT (MwSt)**. The VAT/UID number (CHE-… MWST) will
  be added once available.

## 7. Late payment

Invoices are due within **[14 days]**. If the customer is in default, amai! may
charge **default interest of 5% per annum** (Art. 104 Swiss Code of Obligations)
and a reminder fee of **[CHF 20] per reminder**. After **[two]** unsuccessful
reminders amai! may suspend the service until payment is received and, for material
non-payment, terminate for good cause. *(This reflects common Swiss practice;
confirm the exact figures with your lawyer.)*

## 8. Changes after delivery

Hosting and maintenance are included in the subscription. **Any change requested
after delivery** of the website is billed at **CHF 95 per hour**, **charged in
started quarter-hour units (15 minutes), i.e. CHF 23.75 per started quarter, rounded
up.** Larger requests are estimated in advance on request.

## 9. Ownership and intellectual property

- The customer's **content and domain name** always belong to the customer.
- **Design and code** are provided for use during the subscription. **Ownership of
  the design and code transfers to the customer once the subscription has ended and
  all amounts due have been paid in full** (any outstanding monthly fees, the
  applicable termination fee and any add-on). Until then the customer holds a
  non-exclusive licence to use them for the subscribed website.
- For the **add-on**, the rights to the final logo and brand assets transfer to the
  customer upon full payment of the one-time fee.
- amai! may reference the project as a portfolio item unless the customer objects
  in writing.

## 10. Hosting, maintenance and availability

amai! provides hosting, technical updates, basic security and ongoing maintenance
within the plan. amai! aims for high availability on a best-efforts basis. No
specific uptime/SLA is guaranteed unless agreed separately in writing.

## 11. Customer obligations

The customer ensures that supplied content is lawful and does not infringe third
party rights, and provides timely cooperation. The customer is responsible for the
content it publishes.

## 12. SEO

amai! sets up technical and content SEO foundations. **Specific search engine
rankings or results cannot be guaranteed.**

## 13. Liability

amai! is liable only for direct damage caused by intent or gross negligence.
Liability for slight negligence and for indirect or consequential damage (e.g. lost
profit, data loss, downtime) is excluded as far as legally permitted. Where
liability cannot be excluded, it is **capped at the fees paid by the customer in the
12 months before the event**. amai! is not liable for events outside its control
(force majeure, third party providers, internet outages). *(Confirm cap and wording
with your lawyer.)*

## 14. Data protection

amai! processes personal data in line with its Privacy Policy. Where amai! processes
personal data on behalf of the customer, a separate data processing agreement
applies.

## 15. Changes to these GTC

amai! may amend these GTC. Changes are communicated in advance; if the customer does
not object within **[30 days]**, or continues to use the service, the changes are
deemed accepted. Material changes to the customer's disadvantage give a right to
terminate to the effective date.

## 16. Governing law and jurisdiction

Swiss law applies, excluding conflict-of-law rules and the CISG. The **exclusive
place of jurisdiction is Bern, Switzerland**, subject to mandatory legal venues.
