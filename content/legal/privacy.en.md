## 2. Overview

## 3. What we process, why and on what legal basis

**3.1 Contact, Readiness Check and appointments.** Through our forms we receive your name, email address, company and phone (optional), your message or answers, the service you are interested in, your language, the page and campaign source and your consent. If you book an appointment, our scheduling service sends us your name and email address. We use this to answer your question, schedule a call or deliver a report. Legal basis: steps prior to a contract (Art. 6.1.b) or your consent (Art. 6.1.a).

**3.2 Clients, quotes, invoices and client portal.** For clients we process company and contact details and the content of quotes, invoices, agreements and project documents. If you pay online, Stripe processes the payment. We only receive the payment status and no card details. Legal basis: performance of the contract (Art. 6.1.b) and the statutory accounting obligation (Art. 6.1.c). When you log in to the portal or sign electronically, we record IP address, browser details and time for security and as proof of signature. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f).

**3.3 Relationship management (CRM).** We keep an internal CRM of prospects, clients and partners: name, role, company, business email address and phone, LinkedIn profile where available and a log of our contacts. Sources: you, public business sources and earlier contacts (events, referrals, network). Legal basis: legitimate interest in building and maintaining business relationships (Art. 6.1.f).

**3.4 Business prospecting.** We sometimes contact companies for which our 3D configurators or software may be relevant.

- **Data:** business contact details only (name, role, company, business email address), never private data.
- **Source:** first the company's own website. If it lists no suitable contact person, business contact databases such as Hunter.io or RocketReach. We record the source for each contact.
- **Selection:** if a company lists contact persons with their business email address on its own website, we only write to those in a role that fits our proposal (e.g. management, sales, marketing). If no contact persons are listed, we use a business contact database (Hunter.io or RocketReach) to find a person in such a role or we use the company's general address, such as info@. We never write to more than three people per company.
- **Purpose and legal basis:** a business proposal and at most one follow up, based on legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f). We have assessed beforehand that this interest does not override that of the recipient and we take into account the email marketing rules of the recipient's country.
- **Information:** every message states who we are, where we found the address, a link to this policy and how to unsubscribe.
- **Objection and unsubscribing:** one reply is enough. We delete your data within five working days and put your address on our suppression list so that you are not contacted again.

**3.5 Website, security and spam.** Our hosting providers keep server and security logs (IP address, browser, time, pages requested). A honeypot and Cloudflare Turnstile protect our forms against abuse. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f).

**3.6 Cookies and analytics.** A cookie stores your choice and localStorage remembers your language. This requires no consent. Only with your consent do we set cookies for Google Analytics 4 (analytics), Google Ads (conversion measurement) and the LinkedIn Insight Tag. Without consent Google only receives cookieless, aggregated signals (Consent Mode). You can change your choice at any time via the link at the bottom of the site. Legal basis: consent (Art. 6.1.a).

**3.7 Configurators on client websites.** For configurators we build and host for clients, we process requests from their visitors on behalf of that client. The client is then the controller and SEMANU the processor. The client's privacy policy applies.

Some providers (e.g. Google, Cloudflare, Stripe, UptimeRobot, LinkedIn, RocketReach) may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the US. This takes place under the Standard Contractual Clauses of the European Commission and, where applicable, the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

## 6. Retention periods

- **Requests via forms and Readiness Check:** 12 months after the last contact, unless a client relationship follows
- **Prospecting data without a response:** 12 months after the last message
- **Suppression list:** only the email address, as long as needed to avoid contacting you again
- **CRM data:** as long as the relationship is active, at most 36 months after the last meaningful contact
- **Quotes, invoices and project documents:** the statutory accounting period (10 years)
- **Signed agreements and signing log:** duration of the cooperation plus 5 years
- **Client portal access logs:** 24 months
- **Server and security logs:** about 90 days
- **Google Analytics:** 14 months

## 7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and port your data and you can withdraw consent at any time. You can also always **object** to processing based on legitimate interest. For direct marketing and prospecting we then stop immediately and unconditionally. Email [hello@semanu.eu](mailto:hello@semanu.eu). We reply within one month. You can lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority, Rue de la Presse 35, 1000 Brussels ([dataprotectionauthority.be](https://www.dataprotectionauthority.be)) or with the supervisory authority in your country.

## 8. Security

We protect data with HTTPS, hosting in the EU with a firewall, access control with two factor authentication and regular security updates. We keep a record of processing activities as required by the GDPR.
